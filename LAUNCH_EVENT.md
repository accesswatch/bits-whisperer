## Full Version (Detailed Announcement)

### "Every Voice Heard" — The BITS Whisperer Launch Event

#### Turn Speech Into Text — Privately, Accurately, and Accessibly

**Blind Information Technology Solutions (BITS)** invites you to the
official launch of **BITS Whisperer** — a free, open-source desktop
application that puts the power of audio transcription in your hands,
built from the ground up for accessibility.

---

#### What Is BITS Whisperer?

BITS Whisperer transforms spoken audio into accurate, editable text on
your own computer. Whether you're transcribing meetings, lectures,
interviews, or podcasts, BITS Whisperer gives you 18 transcription
engines — including 5 completely free, offline engines that keep your
audio entirely on your machine. No cloud required. No data collection.
No compromises.

With full keyboard navigation, screen reader support for NVDA and JAWS,
and a menu-bar-first interface, BITS Whisperer was designed so that
*every user* can transcribe with confidence — not as an afterthought,
but as the foundation.

---

#### What to Expect

**Hour 1 — The Launch Presentation**

Join us for a live walkthrough of BITS Whisperer and see what makes it
different:

- **Private by default** — On-device AI transcription with 14 Whisper
  model sizes. Your recordings never leave your machine unless you
  choose otherwise.
- **17 engines, one app** — From free local models for low-end hardware
  to cloud powerhouses like OpenAI, Google, Deepgram, and more. The app
  scans your hardware and recommends the best engine automatically.
- **AI that works for you** — Translate transcripts into 15+ languages,
  summarize as meeting minutes or bullet points, and chat interactively
  with your transcripts using six AI providers — including Ollama, which
  runs entirely on your device for free.
- **Live transcription** — Real-time microphone speech-to-text with
  voice activity detection.
- **Speaker diarization** — Know who said what with support from 10
  cloud providers plus cloud-free local diarization.
- **7 export formats** — Plain text, Markdown, HTML, Word, SRT
  subtitles, VTT, and JSON.
- **A 9-page setup wizard** that guides you through everything: hardware
  scan, model downloads, provider setup, and preferences.

**Hour 2 — Hands-On Installation & Support**

After the presentation, we're opening the floor for one-on-one
assistance. Our team will help you:

- Download and install BITS Whisperer on your Windows or Mac laptop
- Walk through the first-run setup wizard
- Choose the best transcription engine for your hardware
- Configure AI features, export settings, and keyboard shortcuts
- Answer your questions and hear your feedback

**Bring your laptop and headphones** — we'll get you up and running
before you leave the room.
